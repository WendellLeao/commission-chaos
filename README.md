# Commission Chaos

Commission Chaos is an online multiplayer game about a simple job gone completely wrong: grab pickups scattered across the level and deliver them for commission before the timer runs out. Some items fight back, fleeing when you get close, growing too hot to hold, or swapping your position with another player mid-carry, and teammates can slap each other to steal a delivery and pocket the commission themselves. Every match reshuffles the chaos differently, so no two runs ever play the same.
